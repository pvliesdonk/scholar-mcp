    3.5¶

    Book chapters are cited as chapters. When a paper's DOI is a CrossRef book chapter, the paper result names the book it belongs to, with the book's editors and the chapter's pages, and generate_citations writes it as a chapter entry: BibTeX @incollection, CSL-JSON chapter, RIS CHAP. Chapter references in a patent's cited literature no longer report page ranges as ISBNs, read the German Kap. and S. forms, and carry their book when they name one. CrossRef lookups are paced to the public pool's limits. See Upgrading.

    Upgrading¶

    No step is needed. If you watch the logs for CrossRef warnings, a throttled CrossRef lookup now logs crossref_rate_limited without a traceback, where it used to log crossref_error with one (#760).

    Clients: nothing beyond reconnecting. The new fields are additions: chapter_info on paper results, chapter_info.parent_book on get_patent citations. One output changes for the same input: generate_citations writes a CrossRef book chapter as a chapter entry where it used to write an article or the whole book.

    State: the cache keeps everything. The books that chapters belong to are cached alongside the other CrossRef lookups.

    Security posture: none.

    Book chapters cited as chapters¶

    "A chapter in an edited volume is cited as the whole book or as an article, which is wrong in a bibliography" (#593). Semantic Scholar's "coverage of book chapters is weaker than journal articles" (#67), so this release takes the book from CrossRef.

    When enrichment finds that a paper's DOI is a CrossRef book-chapter, the paper gets a chapter_info with citation_source: "crossref": the chapter and book titles, the book's DOI, editors, pages, publisher, place, edition and ISBN, each left out when CrossRef lacks it (#754). This runs wherever paper results are enriched, such as get_paper, get_citations and get_references. CrossRef keeps a book's editors on the book's record rather than the chapter's, so the book is fetched too and cached. The book title is the book's, not the series it appears in. The fields are listed in Enrichment: Book chapters.

    generate_citations then writes that paper as a chapter of its book (#764):

    • BibTeX @incollection, with the book as booktitle, plus editor, pages, publisher, address, edition and isbn where known. @incollection is the one chapter type classic BibTeX and biblatex read the same way.
    • CSL-JSON chapter, with the book as container-title.
    • RIS CHAP, with the book as T2 and its editors as A2.

    A chapter with no author or editor on record is still written as a chapter, without those fields. A paper Semantic Scholar marks as a conference paper stays @inproceedings, because CrossRef files proceedings volumes such as Lecture Notes in Computer Science as books. Nothing needs enabling: generate_citations enriches by default, and with enrich set to false no CrossRef lookup runs, so a chapter is cited as before. See generate_citations.

    What this does not cover:

    • CrossRef has no chapter number, so neither does the entry.
    • A chapter whose CrossRef record does not link to its book is described from its own record, without the book's editors. That was every Elsevier chapter in the sample behind this work; looking the book up by ISBN instead is open as #753.
    • A chapter without a DOI gets no CrossRef lookup. A title search was measured and not built: it found the chapter for 2 of 37 such papers (#745).

    Chapters in a patent's cited literature¶

    get_patent(sections=["citations"]) reads chapter details from each cited reference's text into chapter_info. Three changes:

    • ISBNs are checked. "get_patent's NPL chapter_info.isbn can hold a number that is not an ISBN": a page range and a Wikipedia revision id were reported as ISBNs (#737). An ISBN is now reported only when its check digit is valid (#750).
    • German citations are read. German examiner citations write the chapter and pages as Kap. 1, S. 1-44; these now give the chapter number and page range, as Ch. and pp. do. The book title after In: also stops before an edition statement such as 1st edition, in English and German citations alike (#751).
    • The book is attached. When a reference resolved to a paper and names its chapter, pages or In: book with a valid ISBN, the book's record is added as chapter_info.parent_book (#755). A reference that resolved to no paper keeps its book in the entry's book field, as before.

    See get_patent.

    CrossRef lookups are paced¶

    CrossRef requests used to go out with no pacing, up to ten at a time, while the public pool allows one in flight. Lookups by DOI are now spaced at least 0.2 seconds apart with one in flight, the rate the pool advertises for them (#760), so enriching many papers at once takes longer. A throttled lookup is logged as a throttle, and a DOI CrossRef doesn't know is logged at DEBUG; either way the paper is returned without its CrossRef data, and the next request asks again.

    Fix¶

    batch_resolve sent some Semantic Scholar paper ids to EPO as patent numbers: an id starting with be, ca or de and a digit "comes back as a patent (source_type: "patent") with an EPO error, not as the paper" (#699). A 40-character hex id is now always a paper (#759).
